# Riforni Theme & Design Rules

- Maintain 100% theme consistency across all screens using `AppTheme` from `lib/theme.dart`.
- Primary Color: `AppTheme.primary` (Cobalt Blue `0xFF2563EB`).
- Background: `AppTheme.bgCanvas` (`0xFFF8FAFC`).
- Card/Surface: `Colors.white` (`AppTheme.bgSurface`) with `AppTheme.borderSubtle` (`0xFFE2E8F0`).
- Text: `AppTheme.textMain` (`0xFF0F172A`), `AppTheme.textSecondary` (`0xFF475569`).
- Statuses: WhatsApp `AppTheme.whatsappGreen`, Success `AppTheme.statusSuccess`, Warning `AppTheme.statusWarning`, Danger `AppTheme.statusDanger`.
- Language: Italian ONLY throughout all screens and dialogs.
